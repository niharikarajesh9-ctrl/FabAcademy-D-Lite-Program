Final Project Ideas

I’ve been captivated by the idea of interactive and responsive tech in the past few years but on my own, I could only get so far as creating audio reactive visuals on TouchDesigner. Coming to FabAcademy, I knew, if given the opportunity, I’d like to build upon this concept. So some of my initial ideas revolved around redesigning normal day to day products to bring life to them. I’m an artist and I like to surround myself with beautiful objects, who doesn't? Theres no reason all our tech has to be the same grey black brutalist design. With that in mind I was inspired by Harry Issac’s works, specifically his recent Puff flash drive design, that fascinated me the moment I came across it. Irregularly shaped, its designed to have a small coloured led screen that reacts to any movement of data within the files with small fluid animations appearing on the screen. Not only that it also could be worn as a keychain or necklace. It’s whimsical, strange and fully functional! The idea of making something like this delights me.


Thus my first few ideas were: redesigning a flashdrive to be reactive to movement within the drive This would be almost an exact copy of Puff but I thought since I was only getting introduced to the world of design and tech, it would be a good project to undertake within this course. I’d be able to come up with my own organic shape for the body and code the animations in my own way. As well as understand how I’d go about building something at such a small scale.


A second idea was adjacent to Puff but with my own twist to it redesigning a flashdrive to be a desktop buddy with expressions With this idea, I thought i could make it so that my flashdrive would have small expressions that I was thinking of using eink and instead of abstract animations, making expressions so I’d be able to keep a “sleeping” expression triggered by the eject command so there would be a face even when it isn't plugged into a power source. A third idea was inspired by my love for music and my touchdesigner experiments redesigning an mp3 player to have audio reactive visuals A small cube with a screen (inspired by the very first apple mp3 players) thats only function was to directly connect to Spotify, allowing the listener to play their music and have animations reacting to the bass and beats. A futuristic, captivating design, a replacement to our phones, it would keep anyone entertained.


For my fourth idea, I wanted to come up with something that could have a real positive impact. Something that would help people and make their lives easier. As always, the first person I thought of was my baby brother. He suffers from asthma, and often falls ill. there have been nights when my mother would be scared to fall asleep because she was afraid his o2 levels would drop in his sleep and she wouldn’t be able to tell unless she kept checking incrementally. I thought of making a watch or an unobtrusive band that could be worn around the wrist while sleeping that tracks and logs o2 levels at set intervals on an app. If the levels were to drop past a certain level, sent alerts on the app, making people aware and the wearer aware. Now thinking of this further I see that there are definitely products that exist that do perform this function- like fitness watches, but I realise that since they combine many functions, the price also rises therefore making it inaccessible to the general public. I dont know yet if a product that only performs this task exists yet. Accuracy and reliability of reading would be important factors to consider here as well. Further research is required.


So far, out of all my ideas, redesigning Puff and mp3 player seem the most appealing to me. But I do not know the details of the building process which I will research on and update here.
