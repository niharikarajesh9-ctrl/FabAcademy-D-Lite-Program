My FabAcademy Documentation


About Me




Hello! My name is Niharika Rajesh, and I am 18 years old, welcome to my documentation. I am writing this to document everything from my learning process and ideas to my mistakes and thoughts over the next 10 weeks.
I think it’s a great exercise for me to inculcate into my life and make habit, its been very helpful in solidifying what I've learnt and also in making me articulate the ideas I have. It was here at FabAcademy that I was properly introduced to the concept of open source, and I immediately resonated with the philosophy of knowledge being easily accessible to anyone who is interested. I think I appreciate the importance of this much more after my gap year, during which I had to figure out and teach myself most of the things I wanted to explore, such as TouchDesigner (a node based visual programming software).
Being on my own, detached from any university and without mentors, it was really good documentation, mainly Youtube vidreos and the fact that many of these programs were free and open-source that motivated me and made it possible for me to actually achieve what I wanted to create. I joined this program during the gap year I took right after graduating high school. I wanted a break from the traditional schooling system and wanted time to refine my skills and spend time on my interests that I wasn’t able to explore as they weren’t traditional school subjects. These range from filmmaking and photography to design and art. I also write poetry and the occasional personal essay.
After about eight months of experimenting ion my own, I found myself craving a community of creatives I could bounce ideas off, mentors who could guide me, and a third space where I could learn, experiment, and be around other makers.
